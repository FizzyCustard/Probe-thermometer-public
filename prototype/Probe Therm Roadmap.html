Cook history, favourites & notes

Live prototype layered on top of your existing dashboard. Same dark + pink language; new features added as native extensions, not bolt-ons.

What's new

  • Start / Stop a cook — cook bar at the top; saves a session on stop.
  • Cook Notes — timeline appears during an active cook. Type into the composer, press Enter.
  • Preset Temps — chip row in Settings → Targets, filling the meat field.
  • Favourites — list in Settings; load a config or delete one. Star a past cook to add it.
  • History — second tab; tap any cook to see stats, notes & replay chart.

Try this

  • Tap History → Sunday Brisket for the full detail view.
  • Open Settings to see preset chips & favourites.
  • Hit Start cook, name it, and the Notes timeline appears below the chart.
